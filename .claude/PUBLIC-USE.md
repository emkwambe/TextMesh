# TextMesh Public Use — Product Clarification

> **Key Insight**: TextMesh is public without being algorithmic.

---

## The Clarification

TextMesh is **NOT**:
- ❌ Private-only
- ❌ Invite-only forever
- ❌ Only for educators/SIGs

TextMesh **IS**:
- ✅ Public-facing, but not crowd-driven
- ✅ Open to everyone, but doesn't turn everyone into a performer
- ✅ A calm space for public information sharing

---

## Two Pillars of TextMesh

| Pillar | Description | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **1. Groups (SIG-First)** | Coordination, study groups, cohorts | PMP Study Group, Tutoring Cohort |
| **2. Public Content** | Articles, reviews, explanations for anyone | Computer price review, Policy brief |

**Both are first-class citizens.**

---

## Public Use Cases (Canonical)

### Use Case: Public Article/Review

> "Write an article reviewing computer prices in a calm space, without sifting through vulgar videos or noisy feeds."

This is **perfect TextMesh usage**:

| Aspect | How TextMesh Handles It |
|--------|------------------------|
| Author intent | Share useful information, not chase reactions |
| Reader experience | Calm, readable, no autoplay, no clickbait |
| Discovery | Topic-based, not algorithmic |
| Comments | Clarifications, not reactions |
| Success metric | Usefulness, not virality |

### Use Case: Public Explainer

Someone writes:
- Weekly tech price updates
- Policy summaries
- Learning resources
- How-to guides

Readers find it through:
- Topic following ("Computers", "Tech Prices", "Policy")
- Public groups
- Search
- Direct links

**NOT through**:
- Trending outrage
- Recommendation spirals
- Influencer amplification

## What "General Public" Means on TextMesh

### On Most Platforms
- Random exposure
- Algorithmic discovery
- Viral content
- Mass attention

### On TextMesh
- People who want clear information
- People who want structured updates
- People who want calm participation
- People who don't want to perform to be heard

**The general public is welcome — they just aren't treated as a crowd.**

---

## What the General Public Can Do

### 1. Read Public Content (Without Joining)
- Public groups are readable
- Public posts are accessible
- No infinite scroll pressure
- "Browse useful explanations," not "doomscroll"

### 2. Follow Topics (Not Just People)
- Follow topics: "Technology", "Fitness", "Policy"
- Follow public update groups
- Follow explainers
- Less personality-driven, more information-driven

### 3. Join Open Groups Easily
- News briefs
- Learning communities
- Fitness challenges
- Public study groups
- **Anyone can join — with context, not impulse**

### 4. Create Groups or Post Publicly
- Create public groups
- Publish public posts/articles
- Start journeys
- **Only requirement**: State the purpose clearly

---

## What TextMesh Does NOT Offer (By Design)

- ❌ Infinite viral feeds
- ❌ Engagement-driven popularity contests
- ❌ "Anyone can say anything to everyone" dynamics
- ❌ Fame-through-outrage mechanics

**That's not "nothing" — that's intentional restraint.**

## Public Content Placement Options

### Option A: Public Group (Recommended)

Example: **"PC & Laptop Price Guides"**
- Public visibility
- Readable by anyone
- Posts are article-like
- Comments are clarifications

This becomes a **calm public reference space**.

### Option B: Public Profile with Articles

- Author publishes public posts
- Posts are long-form text cards
- Discovery is slow, steady, topic-based
- Feels like: Blogs, Substack, Knowledge bases

---

## Why This Isn't "Another Blog Platform"

| Platform | Incentive | Problem |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Blogs/Medium | SEO, claps, monetization | Performance pressure distorts tone |
| Social platforms | Video, rage, comments | Chaos, trolls, noise |
| **TextMesh** | Be useful and accurate | Calm, durable, context-first |

**The author's incentive is usefulness, not clickability.**

## Product Implications

This public use case justifies:

| Feature | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Long-form text support | Articles, reviews, guides |
| Edit history | Corrections, updates |
| "Last updated" timestamps | Trust signal |
| Sectioned posts | Structure for longer content |
| Table support (future) | Comparisons, data |
| Pinning authoritative posts | Highlight best content in groups |

## Summary for Claude Code

When building features, remember:

1. **Groups AND public content are both core**
2. **Public posts should support long-form text**
3. **Discovery is topic-based, not algorithmic**
4. **Comments are for clarification, not reaction farming**
5. **No features that reward shock or outrage**

TextMesh serves:
- ✅ SIGs (Groups, coordination)
- ✅ General public (Articles, topics, calm reading)
- ❌ Crowds (viral, algorithmic, attention-driven)
